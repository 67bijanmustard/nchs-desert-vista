Membership

Join our chapter

NCHS membership is open to Desert Vista students who meet the academic and service requirements below. Applications are reviewed each semester.

1
Chinese Language Enrollment
Currently enrolled in Mandarin 3, Mandarin 4, or AP Mandarin at Desert Vista High School
2
Academic Achievement
Minimum 3.5 GPA in Chinese coursework and overall 3.0 cumulative GPA
3
Cultural Activity Requirement
Log 10 cultural activities per year — these can include cultural events, language practice sessions, community outreach, or other chapter-approved activities related to Chinese language and culture
Apply Now

Submit your interest and a chapter officer will follow up with the full application. Applications reviewed on a rolling basis.

Responses are collected via Google Forms and reviewed by chapter officers. You will receive a follow-up email within 5 school days.
